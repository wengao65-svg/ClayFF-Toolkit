# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in ClayFF-Toolkit welcoming and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience,
or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and scientifically honest.
- Critique ideas and results rather than people.
- Give appropriate credit and disclose the origin of code, data, and methods.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them promptly.
- Respect privacy and do not publish confidential or proprietary material.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the
security contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled
as confidentially as reasonably possible.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review channels,
and public events where someone represents the project.
